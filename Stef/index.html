Quick help

How to explore the 3D map

Use these simple mouse controls to look around comfortably, even if this is your first time in a 3D viewer.

⟲

Rotate

Left-click and drag to turn the camera and look around the scene.

✥

Move

Right-click and drag to slide across the map without changing your height.

⇅

Zoom

Scroll up or down to move closer for detail or farther away for overview.

⟷

Drag

Click, hold, and move slowly at first. Small drags make the view easier to control.

Tip: If you use a trackpad, try two-finger drag for panning and pinch to zoom.
